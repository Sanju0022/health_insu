    Sorting customers into risk tiers

    Tiers follow thresholds — smoking, BMI 30, age 45 and 58. Trees find thresholds on their own; KNN and Logistic Regression catch up once those thresholds are added as flags. Naive Bayes stays behind because the flags break its independence assumption.

    The formula

    Quoted premium = Ĉtier × (1 + expense loading + profit margin) + z × σtier

    Risk points = 2 × [smoker] + [BMI ≥ 30] + [age ≥ 45] + [age ≥ 58]  →  0 Low · 1–2 Medium · 3+ High

    Bracketed terms are 1 if true, 0 otherwise; region is relative to east. σ is each tier model's cross-validated error.

    Numbers come from the same trained models as the notebook and the Streamlit app (healthwise_model.joblib), exported to run in the browser.
